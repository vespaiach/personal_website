# Moose Mountain Bike Trail Society (MMBTS)

[mmbts.com](https://www.mmbts.com/) is the website for a nonprofit that builds and maintains mountain bike trails on Moose and Prairie Mountains. I maintain the site, which runs on Squarespace.

Event registration data was a recurring headache, so I also wrote a Google Apps Script. It pulls registrations into a Google Sheet and generates organized reports from it. That replaced manual data wrangling with a repeatable process the organizing team can rely on for each event.
