# Moose Mountain Bike Trail Society (MMBTS)

[mmbts.com](https://www.mmbts.com/) · **Role:** Maintain + built tooling · **Platform:** Squarespace, Google Apps Script

MMBTS is a nonprofit that builds and maintains mountain bike trails on Moose and Prairie Mountains. It needed ongoing support for its public website and a better way to handle event registration data.

## What I did

I maintain the society's Squarespace site, keeping its content and pages up to date.

## The problem I solved

Event registrations came in through Google Forms, and turning the responses into something the organizers could use was manual work, repeated for every event. I built a Google Apps Script that pulls the registration data into a Google Sheet and generates organized reports: participant lists, totals and payments, and volunteer and waiver tracking. The organizing team now has a repeatable, low-maintenance process to rely on for each event.

**Stack:** Squarespace, Google Apps Script, Google Forms, Google Sheets
