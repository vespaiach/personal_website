# Sol Mountain Lodge

[solmountain.com](https://solmountain.com/) · **Role:** Maintain · **Platform:** Squarespace 7.1

Sol Mountain Lodge is a backcountry ski and hiking lodge in BC's Monashee Mountains.

## What I did

I maintain the site post-launch, handling content updates, seasonal changes, and fixes.

## The problem I solved

The lodge's availability and booking table needed to appear on several pages, but Squarespace has no native way to define a block once and have it update everywhere. Every edit meant updating each page by hand and hoping nothing drifted out of sync. Now the availability data lives on a single Squarespace page, and a script injected across the relevant pages fetches it live and renders the table wherever it's needed. Lodge staff make one edit, and every page shows it.

**Stack:** Squarespace, JavaScript
