# AbSPORU (Alberta SPOR SUPPORT Unit)

[absporu.ca](https://absporu.ca/) is the public website for the Alberta SPOR SUPPORT Unit, a health research organization that connects patients, clinicians, and researchers across Alberta's patient-oriented research network. It's a WordPress site built with Elementor. The content is varied: training and event listings, patient-partner opportunities, research service applications, and navigation for researchers, patients, and policy-makers.

I maintain it on an ongoing basis: publishing and updating content, building and adjusting Elementor pages, keeping plugins, theme, and core up to date, and fixing issues as they come up. I also keep the front end fast and accessible, and easy for non-technical staff to update themselves.
