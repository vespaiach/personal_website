# SMiiL Heights

[smiil.ca](https://www.smiil.ca/) · **Role:** Built · **Platform:** Webflow

SMiiL Heights is a dental hygiene clinic in Crescent Heights, Calgary.

## What I did

I built the clinic's website in Webflow. The site brings its warm, mountain-inspired brand to life in a clean, mobile-friendly experience that walks visitors through services, FAQs, and booking with an approachable, patient-first feel.

**Stack:** Webflow
