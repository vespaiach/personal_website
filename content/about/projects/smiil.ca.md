# SMiiL Heights — Website Development (Webflow)

[smiil.ca](https://www.smiil.ca/) - I built the website for SMiiL Heights, a dental hygiene clinic in Crescent Heights, Calgary, in Webflow. The site brings the clinic's warm, mountain-inspired brand to life in a clean, mobile-friendly experience that walks visitors through services, FAQs, and booking with an approachable, patient-first feel.
